      What you can do here

      • Response Ranking — rank drugs within any cell line, cell lines within any drug, and browse the full drug × cell-line response heatmap.
      • Compare — put two conditions side by side: shared up/down genes, shared pathways, and distance in response-signature space.
      • Signature Search — enter an up/down gene signature and find the conditions whose measured response matches it.
      • Data — download the underlying validated tables (Parquet/CSV) with provenance.
      Scope & limits. Response = L2 magnitude of the truncated top-N log2 fold-change signature (not a full transcriptome). Cell-level expression was not used (only 1 of 3388 shards is public), so single-cell heterogeneity and responder fractions are not provided. Multi-dose curves are assembled across plate triplets; the DMSO origin is synthetic. See Data for the full methods and limitations.
