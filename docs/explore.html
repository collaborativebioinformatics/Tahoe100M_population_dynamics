Response strength is the robust magnitude of the pseudobulk differential-expression signature (treated vs. matched DMSO). It is not drug sensitivity, viability, killing, or clinical efficacy.

How to read this. Rank uses robust RMS log2 fold-change — the root-mean-square of winsorized log2FC across all tested genes for a condition, so ranking is not biased by how many genes passed expression filtering. A replicate reliability badge shows the cosine agreement of the two replicate plates for that cell line × drug × dose, when both were measured. High response magnitude means the cells' transcriptome moved a lot; it does not tell you whether the cells died.
